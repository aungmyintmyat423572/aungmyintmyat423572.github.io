Early Life and Education of Myint Myat

Date of Birth & Birthplace: Myint Myat was born Myo Myint Myat on August 7, 1986, in Yangon, Myanmar. Family Background: He is the youngest of three siblings, born to parents U Myint Aung and Daw Kyi Kyi Win. Education: He was raised and educated in Yangon, and later graduated with a bachelor's degree from East Yangon University.

career

Career Beginnings: Driven by a passion for the arts since his youth, he officially entered the entertainment industry around 2006 as a model in his early twenties. He gradually worked his way up by playing supporting characters and antagonist (villain) roles in local direct-to-video films before rising to mainstream stardom as a top-tier leading actor.
